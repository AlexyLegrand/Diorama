# Asset credits

Every asset that is not made by us must be listed here with its source and license before it is committed.
Prefer **CC0** (public domain, no attribution required) so the game can be shared and sold freely.

## Third-party assets in the game

| Asset | Author / source | License |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/fonts/LilitaOne-Regular.ttf` | Juan Montoreano, Google Fonts | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/fonts/OFL.txt`, must ship with the game) |
| raylib (engine library) | Ramon Santamaria and contributors | zlib/libpng |

All other models, animations, textures and icons are made for Diorama in Blender.
Music and sound effects are generated for Diorama: check the generator's terms before any commercial release.
